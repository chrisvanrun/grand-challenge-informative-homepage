High-impact challenges

All challenges

PI-CAI

The Lancet Oncology, 2024

Detection of clinically significant prostate cancer on biparametric MRI.

Compared against 62 radiologists from 20 countries reading the same cases.

autoPET

Nature Machine Intelligence, 2024

Segmentation of metabolically active tumour lesions on whole-body FDG PET/CT.

Organised from Tübingen and LMU Munich; over 350 teams registered from every continent.

TIGER

Nature Communications, 2026

Automated scoring of tumour-infiltrating lymphocytes in HER2-positive and triple-negative breast cancer.

Benchmarked on 3,708 cases from clinical practice and phase 3 trials, with computational scores agreeing closely with pathologists.
