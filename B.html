Reader studies

Collect datasets with expert clinical reads, ready to use as training data or as a reference standard.

  • Training data — create annotated datasets, or build multi-reader consensus ground truth.
  • Model evaluation — compare your algorithm against clinical readers on the same cases.

Readers take part from anywhere, in the browser, with no install — so you can recruit across hospitals and countries without moving any data. The viewer handles everything from DICOM and whole-slide images to video and text reports.

Challenges

Benchmark AI algorithms against each other on the same clinical task.

  • Take part — pick an open challenge, submit your algorithm, see where it lands on the leaderboard.
  • Host your own — put your clinical question to the field and find the best AI model to solve it. Your test set stays private: submitted models run on the platform, so participants never see the data.
Browse challenges Host your own challenge

Algorithms

Run a trained model on your own cases, straight from the browser.

  • Try one — upload your own cases to a published algorithm and see the result. No install, no code.
  • Publish your own — let others run your model on their own cases without handing over your code or weights. Invite individual users, open it to all verified users, or make it public.

TIGER

Nature Communications, 2026

Automated scoring of tumour-infiltrating lymphocytes in HER2-positive and triple-negative breast cancer.

Benchmarked on 3,708 cases from clinical practice and phase 3 trials, with computational scores agreeing closely with pathologists.
